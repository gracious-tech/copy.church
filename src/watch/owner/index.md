<div class="disclaimer">

_Only modern translations published within the last 95 years are included._

_The organization may own the copyright, or may simply have been delegated responsibility for
licensing on the owner's behalf._

_Most Bibles do have a public license allowing a limited number of verses to be quoted. However,
these licenses rarely go much beyond what fair use already allows, and some are even more
restrictive than fair use. Instead, we report whether a Bible version can be shared in its entirety._

_We aim for accuracy based on available information, but mistakes are possible —
[contact us](/about/#contact) if you spot one._
