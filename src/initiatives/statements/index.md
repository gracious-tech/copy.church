# Christian Statement Ratings

Christian statements are proclamations of beloved truths, written to be read, shared, taught, and embraced by the Church. However, many of these statements [remain owned by their publisher](https://sellingjesus.org/articles/statements) rather than the Church, who alone decides how they can be shared.
